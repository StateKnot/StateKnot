## Spec

**[P2] Scope the duplicate no-policy promise to the same committed attempt receipt.**

The new EN RFC acceptance text (line 248) says that “refusal/duplicate recovery”
performs no business send or second policy call. The CN counterpart (line 92)
has the same broad promise. Fresh recovery GetTask queries require current policy;
attested MessageIdReplay requires authorization of its newly prepared actual
request and may issue its configured business send. This differs from rereading
or recovering the acknowledgement of the same committed durable Failed/Unknown
attempt receipt. The adjacent text correctly requires current-policy checks, so
these two claims need an explicit distinction.

Separate these three cases: refusal sends no business request; duplicate retrieval/
ack recovery of the same committed attempt receipt creates no new send or second
policy evaluation; a fresh probe/attested replay reauthorizes and may send under
its existing contract. No code or retry-policy change is requested.

Other reviewed requirements have no actionable finding: HTTP GET/DELETE may
have no body whereas JSON-RPC reads use a POST envelope; replay keeps the business
message identity while fresh RPC rounds allocate correlation IDs, so different
transport envelopes must not be required byte-equal. Existing Debug identity,
tenant/task/scope/attempt fields remain visible, and body/URL access is intentional
release to trusted host code. Clone may retain the payload. Facts are not policy,
consent, issuer verification or universal header authorization. RFC acceptance,
R1 closure, compilation and production qualification remain open.

The original five v1 documents, their baseline snapshots and complete diff are
